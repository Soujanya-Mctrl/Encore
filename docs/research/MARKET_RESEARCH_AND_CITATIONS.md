# Market Research, Competitive Landscape & Citations

## 1. Key Market Evidence (With Methodological Caveats)

1. **Listener Demand for AI Transparency:**
   - **Deezer / Ipsos (Nov 2026):** `80%` of respondents want AI music labeled, including AI-assisted work `[1]`.
   - **Reuters (Nov 2026, 9,000 adults in 8 countries):** `72%` of streaming users want to know when AI tracks are recommended `[2]`.
   - **Deezer / Ipsos Blind-Listening Study (2025):** `97%` of participants did not correctly distinguish fully generated from human-made music in that test `[3]`. *(Supports informed discovery; does not measure classifier accuracy.)*
   - **Deezer Upload Report (Apr 2026):** Fully generated music accounted for `44%` of new uploads on Deezer `[4]`. *(Platform upload share, not total catalog/listening share or lost human royalties.)*
2. **Independent Artist Income & AI Adoption:**
   - **Ditto Music Survey (2026, 5,039 independent artists in 112 countries):** `74%` earned below `$1,000` from music in the prior year; `67%` cite social video as their biggest source of new fans; `47%` already use AI in production `[7]`. *(Recruited via Ditto channels; do not generalize to all artists.)*
3. **Platform & Legal Landscape:**
   - **Bandcamp (Jan 2026):** Prohibits music generated wholly or substantially by AI `[8]`.
   - **Suno & ElevenLabs:** Commercial rights depend on plan and timing (Suno paid rights not retroactive by default `[9]`; ElevenLabs Music Terms restrict external libraries/model resale `[10]`, `[11]`).
   - **US Copyright Office (2025):** Distinguishes human-authored expression from purely generated material; provider commercial permission != copyright ownership; US guidance is not a global legal rule `[12]`.

## 2. Falsifiable Product Hypotheses

- **H1 (Comprehension):** Listeners given role-specific origin disclosures and evidence status understand who made a track better than listeners given a single binary AI label.
- **H2 (Intentional Support):** Listeners who value different creation methods will intentionally direct support toward chosen creators when shown disclosure + simple checkout.
- **H3 (Creator Fairness):** Artists prefer transparent, challengeable classification over opaque binary detection.
- **H4 (Pass Utility):** Creators can offer useful, transferable supporter benefits without selling speculative royalty interests.
